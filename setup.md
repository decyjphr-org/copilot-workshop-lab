# Workshop setup

Complete these steps before starting the *Own your Copilot* workshop.
You can read this setup page without signing in to GitHub. Signing in is
required to access your workshop organization and use the workshop tools.

## 1. Have your participant details ready

Your facilitator will provide the following information privately:

| Field | What it contains |
|-------|------------------|
| `GitHub_Org` | Your workshop's GitHub organization |
| `Username` | Your assigned username |
| `GitHub_Handle` | Your GitHub username |
| `Email_Address` | The email address to use when signing in |
| `Password/TAP` | Your password or Temporary Access Pass (TAP) |

Replace the placeholders below with the values you were given. Keep your
password or TAP private; do not post it in this repository or on this page.

## 2. Sign in to your workshop organization

1. Open `https://github.com/<GitHub_Org>` in your browser, replacing
   `<GitHub_Org>` with the organization name provided by your facilitator.
2. When prompted to sign in, enter your `<Email_Address>`.
3. When prompted for a password, enter your `<Password>`. If you were given
   a TAP instead, use it when prompted by your organization's sign-in flow.
4. Complete any additional sign-in prompts and confirm that you can access
   your workshop organization. If you cannot sign in, contact your facilitator.

## 3. Complete Lab 0: Prerequisites and setup

Go to [README -> Lab 0: Prerequisites and setup](https://github.com/decyjphr-org/copilot-workshop-lab/blob/main/README.md#lab-0-prerequisites-and-setup)
and complete the exercises before proceeding to Lab 1.

Download a PDF version of the slides using the link your facilitator provides.
